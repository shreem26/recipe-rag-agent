Chicken Tikka Masala

A creamy, spiced North Indian-inspired curry with grilled marinated chicken.
Serves 4. Prep time: 20 minutes (plus 1 hour marinating). Cook time: 30 minutes.

Ingredients:
- 500 g chicken breast, cubed
- 1 cup yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp paprika
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tomato, pureed
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp butter
- salt to taste
- fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish

Instructions:
1. Combine chicken with yogurt, lemon juice, half the garam masala, cumin,
   and paprika. Marinate at least 1 hour (overnight is better).
2. Grill or pan-sear the marinated chicken until charred and cooked through.
   Set aside.
3. In a large pot, melt butter and saute onion until soft. Add garlic and
   ginger, cook 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Stir in tomato puree and remaining garam masala. Simmer 10 minutes.
5. Add heavy cream and simmer another 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
6. Add the cooked chicken back into the sauce and simmer 5 more minutes.
7. Season with salt, garnish with cilantro, and serve with rice or naan.

Notes: For a dairy-free version, use coconut cream instead of heavy cream and
coconut yogurt for the marinade. For a lighter version, swap heavy cream for
Greek yogurt stirred in off the heat.
