Classic Chocolate Cake

A rich, moist chocolate layer cake perfect for birthdays and celebrations.
Serves 8. Prep time: 25 minutes. Bake time: 35 minutes.

Ingredients:
- 2 cups flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 3/4 cup cocoa powder
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1.5 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 egg
- 1 cup milk
- 0.5 cup vegetable oil
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup hot water

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350F (175C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder,
   baking soda, and salt.
3. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Beat on medium speed for 2 minutes.
4. Stir in hot water last (batter will be thin -- that's normal).
5. Divide batter evenly between pans. Bake 30-35 minutes until a toothpick
   comes out clean.
6. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks to cool completely
   before frosting.

Notes: For a dairy-free version, substitute the milk with almond milk or oat
milk. For a sugar-free version, replace sugar with an equal amount of a
granulated erythritol/monk fruit blend and reduce the hot water slightly since
sugar substitutes don't add moisture the same way.
