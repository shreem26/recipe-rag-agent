Quick Vegetable Stir-Fry

A fast weeknight stir-fry loaded with crisp vegetables in a savory garlic-soy
sauce. Serves 3. Prep time: 10 minutes. Cook time: 10 minutes.

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 1 cup snap peas
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water
- cooked rice, for serving

Instructions:
1. Heat vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
2. Add garlic and stir-fry 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add carrot and broccoli first (they take longest), stir-fry 2 minutes.
4. Add bell pepper and snap peas, stir-fry 2-3 more minutes until
   vegetables are crisp-tender.
5. Whisk together soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil. Pour into the pan.
6. Add the cornstarch slurry and toss until the sauce thickens and coats
   the vegetables, about 1 minute.
7. Serve immediately over rice.

Notes: For gluten-free, use tamari instead of soy sauce. For vegan, replace
honey with maple syrup. Swap in any vegetables you have on hand -- this
recipe is very forgiving.
