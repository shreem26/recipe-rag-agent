Fluffy Banana Pancakes

Soft, naturally sweetened pancakes that use ripe bananas instead of a lot of
added sugar. Serves 3 (about 9 pancakes). Prep time: 10 minutes. Cook time:
15 minutes.

Ingredients:
- 1.5 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 2 banana, mashed
- 1.25 cups milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- butter, for the pan

Instructions:
1. Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas, then whisk in milk, egg, melted
   butter, and vanilla.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just
   combined -- a few lumps are fine, don't overmix.
4. Heat a griddle or nonstick pan over medium heat and lightly butter it.
5. Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on the surface,
   about 2-3 minutes, then flip and cook 1-2 more minutes.
6. Serve warm with maple syrup and extra banana slices.

Notes: For a dairy-free version, use oat milk and a plant-based butter. For a
gluten-free version, swap in a 1:1 gluten-free flour blend. Very ripe,
spotty bananas give the best natural sweetness so you can use less added
sugar.
