Classic Caprese Salad

A simple Italian salad that lets ripe summer tomatoes shine. Serves 4 as a
side. Prep time: 10 minutes. No cook time.

Ingredients:
- 4 tomato, sliced
- 1 mozzarella, sliced (about 8 oz fresh mozzarella)
- fresh basil leaves
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Arrange alternating slices of tomato and mozzarella on a platter.
2. Tuck fresh basil leaves between the slices.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic vinegar.
4. Season with salt and pepper just before serving.

Notes: For a dairy-free version, use a plant-based mozzarella alternative or
simply skip the cheese and add sliced avocado for creaminess. Best made with
in-season, room-temperature tomatoes -- refrigeration dulls their flavor.
